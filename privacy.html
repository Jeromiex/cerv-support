Privacy Policy

CERV. — Effective Date: October 25, 2026

CERV. ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the CERV. mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

CERV. is designed with privacy in mind. Your saved articles (Vault), reading history, and discovery preferences are stored locally on your device. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, only basic profile identifiers you authorize are used to personalize your local profile.

2. Third-Party Services

  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia API: Used to fetch educational articles, summaries, and images based on topics you explore.
  • AI Insights (Google Gemini API): When you ask CERV AI a question about an article, the question and article context are sent to generate a response. No personal identity information is shared.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

You may clear your saved Vault, history, or account preferences at any time inside the app or by uninstalling the application from your device.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at cervappsupport@gmail.com.
