Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I save articles to my Vault? Tap the bookmark/save button on any card or article to store it in your Vault for quick access.
  • How do I adjust my topic discovery preferences? Open Discovery Preferences in the app to slide between Hidden Gems, Balanced, and Global Icons.
  • How do I delete my data? Your saved Vault items and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can clear your data anytime within the app settings or by uninstalling the app.
